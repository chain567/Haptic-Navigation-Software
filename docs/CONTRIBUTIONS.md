# My role in VibeGuide

VibeGuide was a four-person EG 1004 course project at NYU Tandon in Fall 2025. I originated the idea and served as the software/coding lead, working from the initial concept through feature iteration, hardware integration, and the final demonstration.

## Concept and feature development

I defined how navigation information should become physical feedback and worked through the features step by step. This included the left/right device behavior, distance-based vibration levels, warning cues, and controls used to demonstrate the prototype. I evaluated each addition and guided adjustments as the system took shape.

## Hardware integration

I researched Bluetooth options, sourced components from the lab, and participated hands-on throughout circuit assembly. I completed approximately half of the soldering and worked with the team to connect the app, controller, and vibration hardware. The circuit, enclosure, and overall course deliverable were team work.

## Testing and presentation

I was responsible for the final simple functional test, where we confirmed that the connected prototype's basic navigation-to-vibration workflow worked. I also delivered the main technical explanation in our presentation.

## Development approach

I used AI coding tools during implementation, directing the work feature by feature and checking the generated code's behavior on the prototype.

## Project timeline

The original project was built in Fall 2025. In 2026, I organized the original Android source, Arduino sketch, photograph, and documentation for my portfolio.
